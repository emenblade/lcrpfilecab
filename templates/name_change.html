Change of Name Request

This form is to be used to request a formal change of name, for approval by any judge of the state. This form shall affect all legal documentation, including identification and banking details. Applicants must provide reasoning for the change of name, and must state if they have any prior criminal history, and any outstanding warrants. Name changes may not be granted should there be any felonies committed by the requesting individual in the last 14 days.
