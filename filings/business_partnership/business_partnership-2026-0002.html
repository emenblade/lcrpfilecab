This partnership is structured as a General Partnership (GP) in accordance with Governing Law.

This agreement has an effective start date of july 10 and shall continue in perpetuity.

The Partners mentioned above are each referred to herein as a “Partner” and collectively, as the “Partners”. The Partners agree as follows:

Voting

  1. Voting Determination: The value of each vote shall be equal amongst all Partners.
  2. Changes to the Partnership: Any changes made to the Partnership shall require a unanimous vote of the Partners.

Changes to the Partnership shall include, but not be limited to, transferring ownership of Partner interest, removing the General Partner(s), dissolution of the Partnership and any other legal, structural, employment or organizational amendments.

  1. Accounting Audits: All Partnership accounting records shall be audited upon a unanimous vote of the Partners.

Partner Duties

  1. Capital Contributions: There are no Capital Contributions that are or will be made to the Partnership as part of this Agreement.
  2. Costs and Expenses: The costs and expenses of the Partnership shall be the responsibility of all the partners equally.
  3. Conflict of Interest: The Partners shall be able to engage in similar business activities.
  4. Management: All the Partners are responsible for managing the day to day operations of the Partnership.
  5. Work Requirements: All of the partners are required to work for the Partnership. Compensation, if any, shall be determined in a separate document.
  6. Voluntary Withdrawal: If any Partner should withdraw from the Partnership, they must give at least 7 days written notice to the Partnership. Such withdrawal shall have no effect on the day to day operations of the Partnership.

Organizational Matters

  1. Profit Distribution: Profits of the Partnership shall be distributed and paid as an equal amount to each Partner from the Date of the Agreement.
  2. Existing Funds: Any funds already accumulated in the Business prior to the start of this Agreement are protected from the distribution of funds.
  3. First Right of Refusal: If any Partner shall enter into an agreement to sell their ownership in the Partnership with an individual or entity that is not a current Partner, the Partnership must be given a first right of refusal — the Partnership shall be given 7 days notice to purchase the ownership interest under the same terms agreed upon by the potential buyer.
  4. Regular Meetings: The Partnership shall have meetings when requested by the Partners.
  5. Accounting Methods: Accounting records shall be kept on an accrual basis.

Involuntary Withdrawal

The involuntary withdrawal of a Partner shall include, but not be limited to, the following:

  1. Death of a Partner;
  2. Partner that becomes incapacitated or not able to make decisions on their own as determined by a licensed physician;
  3. Incompetence or negligence of a Partner;
  4. A Partner's breach of fiduciary duties;
  5. A criminal or civil judgment made against a Partner that impacts or involves the Business; and
  6. Any other action made by a Partner that constitutes a breach of this Agreement.

Dissolution

In the event of a dissolution of the Partnership, each Partner will share equally in any remaining assets or liabilities of the Partnership on an equal basis.

Banking

All funds generated by the Partnership shall be deposited into bank accounts known and approved by the Partners. Such funds shall not be commingled with any other funds or placed in an account of another individual or entity.

Force Majeure

A Partner will be free of liability under this Agreement if their performance is hindered due to an event that the Partnership could not have anticipated and is out of its control. The Partner(s) so affected shall use all reasonable efforts to avoid or remove such causes of nonperformance and if impacted must provide immediate written notice to the Partnership including any actions to cure said issue.

Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed under the laws located in the State of San Andreas.

Severability

In the event any section, provision, or part of this Agreement is found to be invalid or unenforceable, only that specific language or part so found, and not the entire Agreement, will be inoperative.

Disputes

The Partners agree to resolve any disputes arising out of this Agreement through polite interactions and negotiations. If any dispute is not able to be resolved by negotiation within 14 days, the Partners involved shall enter mediation with a neutral party.

If said mediation is not successful in resolving the dispute or is not applicable, any outstanding issues will be submitted to final and binding arbitration under the San Andreas District Court. The Arbitrator's award shall be final, and judgment will be entered upon it by the Court.

Entire Agreement

This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Partners with respect to the subject matter hereof and supersedes all other prior agreements and understandings, both written and oral, between the Partners with respect to the subject matter hereof.
