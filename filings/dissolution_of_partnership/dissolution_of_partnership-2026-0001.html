Appendix

Involuntary Withdrawal

The involuntary withdrawal of a Partner shall include, but not be limited to, the following:

  1. Death of a Partner;
  2. Partner that becomes incapacitated or not able to make decisions on their own as determined by a licensed physician;
  3. Incompetence or negligence of a Partner;
  4. A Partner's breach of fiduciary duties;
  5. A criminal or civil judgment made against a Partner that impacts or involves the Business; and
  6. Any other action made by a Partner that constitutes a breach of this Agreement.

Dissolution

In the event of a dissolution of the Partnership, each Partner will share equally in any remaining assets or liabilities of the Partnership on an equal basis. There are three types of Dissolution:

  1. The Business will now operate under a single owner
  2. The Partnership will be sold to a new Partner
  3. The Business will cease to operate

Banking

All funds generated by the Partnership up to the point of Dissolution will be required to be divided equally amongst the Partners (assets and liabilities).
