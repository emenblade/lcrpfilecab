San Andreas Penal Code

Last Call RP • Department of Justice

The San Andreas Penal Code is a guideline for sentencing. A Justice may deviate from it based on the specifics of a case, but not excessively, as that would breach the defendant’s 8th Amendment rights.

Drugs and Alcohol
CC-100 Possession of a Controlled Dangerous Substance Misdemeanor 5mo $5,000 Possess any amount up to 28g of Marijuana, any amount up to 5 Marijuana seeds, any amount up to 10g of Methamphetamine or any amount up to 10g of Cocaine. Possesses prescription medication or other controlled substances without a prescription.
CC-102 Felony Possession of a Controlled Dangerous Substance Felony 30mo $20,000 Possess more than 28g of Marijuana (1-3 Plants), more than 5 Marijuana seeds, more than 10g of Methamphetamine or more than 10g of Cocaine. Possesses 7+ doses of prescription medication or other controlled substances without a prescription.
CC-103 Drug Trafficking HUT Felony — — Transports, distributes, or cultivates large quantities of illegal substances. For this crime, large quantities are considered 200g+ marijuana, 4+ marijuana plants, 200g+ methamphetamine, 100g+ of Cocaine, or 19+ doses of other prescriptions or controlled substances. This is a Hold Until Trial Charge!
CC-104 Sale of a Controlled Dangerous Substance Felony 30mo $40,000 Sells or Distributes a Controlled Dangerous Substance without legal authorization or licensing.
CC-105 Production of a Controlled Dangerous Substance Felony 45mo $80,000 Actively produces or attempts to produce a controlled dangerous substance with the proper medical licenses. [Methamphetamine, Cocaine, Marijuana, Prescriptions, Other Controlled Substances]
CC-106 Cultivation of Marijuana Felony 45mo $60,000 Tending to 1 or more marijuana plants.
CC-120 Underage Drinking Misdemeanor — $5,000 Consumption, purchase, or possession of alcohol by any individual under the age of 21.
CC-121 Public Intoxication Misdemeanor 5mo $5,000 Any individual who is intoxicated over the legal limit of 0.08% while in public and behaves in a manner that is reasonably disturbing to the public/public space, or is a hazard to themselves or others.
CC-122 Furnishing Alcohol to a Minor Misdemeanor 15mo $15,000 Sells or furnishes alcohol to someone under the legal drinking age of 21. If found guilty, results in the revocation of liquor licenses of involved individual(s) and associated organization(s).
Injury / Harm
CC-150 Assault Misdemeanor 5mo $10,000 Intentionally engages in a physical act that causes another person to fear imminent harm or offensive contact.
CC-151 Battery Misdemeanor 15mo $16,000 Intentionally engages in physical non-consensual contact that results in harm or offense to another person.
CC-152 Battery against an LEO Misdemeanor 30mo $130,000 Commits Battery against an LEO during the lawful execution of the LEO's duties. Must be used in place of Battery when appropriate.
CC-153 Felony Assault Felony 15mo $20,000 Intentionally engages in a physical act that causes another person to fear imminent death OR commits assault while using a deadly weapon.
CC-154 Felony Battery Felony 30mo $30,000 Intentionally engages in a physical act that causes severe bodily harm to another person without the intent to kill OR committing battery while using a deadly weapon.
CC-155 Felony Battery against an LEO Felony 60mo $250,000 Commits felony battery against an LEO during the lawful execution of the LEO's duties. Must be used in place of felony battery when appropriate.
CC-156 Involuntary Manslaughter Felony 60mo $80,000 A person unintentionally causes another’s death through criminal negligence or while committing a dangerous misdemeanor or infraction.
CC-157 Manslaughter Felony 120mo $400,000 A person unintentionally causes another’s death by an intentional act, a felony act, or acts committed during a separate dangerous felony
CC-160 Attempted Murder of a Gov. Employee Felony 40mo $40,000 The attempted intentional killing of a person who is employed by the government.
CC-161 Attempted Second Degree Murder Felony 120mo $60,000 Attempts to cause the death of a person with the intention of killing, but that is not premeditated or planned.
CC-162 Second Degree Murder Felony 160mo $200,000 Causes the death of a person with the intention of killing, but that is not premeditated or planned.
CC-163 Second Degree Murder of a Government Employee HUT Felony — $2,000,000 Causes the death of a government employee with the intention of killing, but is not premeditated or planned. This is a Hold Until Trial Charge!
CC-164 Attempted First Degree Murder Felony 160mo $250,000 The attempted intentional killing of another person by someone who has acted willfully, deliberately, and with planning and malice aforethought.
CC-165 First Degree Murder HUT Felony — — The intentional killing of another person by someone who has acted willfully, deliberately, and with planning and malice aforethought. This is a Hold Until Trial Charge!
CC-166 First Degree Murder of a Government Employee HUT Felony — — The intentional killing of a government employee by someone who has acted willfully, deliberately, and with planning and malice aforethought. This is a Hold Until Trial Charge!
CC-167 Attempted Mass Murder of Government Employees HUT Felony — $1,800,000 The attempted intentional killing of 5+ persons who are employed by the government. This includes SASP, SAMS, DOJ, DAO, and Rangers. This is a Hold Until Trial Charge!
CC-170 False Imprisonment Misdemeanor 5mo $10,000 Keeps a person in an area or restrains them for an extended time against their will.
CC-171 Imprisonment of a Gov. Employee Felony 15mo $25,000 Holds a Government Employee in an area or restrains them for an extended period of time against their will.
CC-172 Kidnapping Felony 15mo $15,000 Takes a person against their will and abducts them without consent.
CC-173 Kidnapping of a Gov. Employee Felony 120mo $25,000 Takes a Gov. Employee against their will and abducts them without consent. Gov. Employees include DOJ, DAO, PDO, SASP, Rangers, and SAMS.
CC-174 Hit and Run Misdemeanor 10mo $10,000 Flees from a motor-vehicle accident without identifying themselves to the other party involved in the accident.
CC-175 Felony Hit and Run Felony 25mo $120,000 Flees from a motor-vehicle accident that results in serious injury.
CC-176 Stalking Felony 20mo $75,000 A pattern of repeated and unwanted attention, harassment, contact, or any other course of conduct directed at a specific person that would cause a reasonable person to feel fear.
CC-177 Harassment Misdemeanor 10mo $7,500 Engages in a course of vexatious comment or conduct that is known or ought reasonably to be known to be unwelcome.
CC-178 Torture Felony 200mo $100,000 Hurts someone with intent to cause cruel, prolonged, or extreme pain and suffering for revenge, extortion, persuasion, or sadistic purposes.
CC-179 Mayhem HUT Felony — $1,200,000 Tortures or inflicts extreme emotional distress on the same person or group over 3+ days. Separate days must be within 10 days. Cannot be combined with CC160 for the same inflictions. A law enforcement agency does not fall under the definition of group. This is a Hold Until Trial Charge!
CC-180 Extortion Felony 20mo $4,000 Uses threats of future physician, emotional, or other harm for personal or financial gain.
CC-181 Criminal Threats Misdemeanor 5mo $2,500 Intentionally threatening to kill or seriously harm someone else, which causes reasonable fear.
CC-182 Brandishing Misdemeanor 5mo $3,500 Displays a weapon in public without a legally justified reason.
CC-183 Gang Related Violence Misdemeanor 15mo $10,000 Any person associated with a gang, motorcycle club, or criminal group who intentionally uses force or violence against another individual based on affiliation.
CC-184 Gang Related Shooting Felony 30mo $20,000 Any person associated with a gang, motorcycle club, or criminal group who discharges a weapon against another individual based on affiliation.
CC-185 Reckless Endangerment Misdemeanor 5mo $3,000 Engages in conduct that creates a substantial risk of physical injury or death to another person.
CC-190 Terrorism HUT Felony — — Acts of deadly violence or intimidation intended to coerce civilians, influence government, or advance an ideology. Requires evidence of both harmful acts and political/ideological motivation. This is a Hold Until Trial Charge.
Property
CC-200 Petty Theft Misdemeanor 5mo $10,000 The act of taking money or property without permission or legal right from a person or property.
CC-201 Grand Theft Felony 20mo $50,000 As theft, but a property value of 20000+ dollars. Does not apply to Theft of a Motor Vehicle, Vessel, Aircraft, or Grand Theft Auto.
CC-202 Theft of a Motor Vehicle Misdemeanor 5mo $3,000 Possesses a vehicle without the consent of the owner or the legal right to possess it without the intention of depriving the owner of the vehicle permanently.
CC-203 Grand Theft Auto Felony 10mo $13,500 Possesses a vehicle without the consent of the owner or the legal right to possess it with the intent of permanently depriving the owner of the vehicle.
CC-204 First Degree Robbery Felony 30mo $15,000 Steals money or property through force or threats of force from a Financial Institution or Government Facility.
CC-205 Second Degree Robbery Felony 20mo $12,000 Steals money or property through force or threats of force from a person or business.
CC-206 Vandalism Misdemeanor 5mo $3,900 Intentionally damaging or destroying property.
CC-207 Arson Felony 10mo $13,000 The act of deliberately setting fire to property.
CC-210 Trespassing Misdemeanor 10mo $2,500 Enters private property without consent, or remains on private property after being asked to leave by the owner.
CC-211 Felony Trespassing Felony 20mo $9,690 As trespassing, but when the trespassed area is restricted and on government property
CC-212 Second Degree Burglary Misdemeanor 10mo $7,800 Entering a property without consent or legal right in order to commit a crime.
CC-213 First Degree Burglary Felony 25mo $12,000 As burglury, but entering a government property, financial institution, or occupied dwelling.
CC-214 Tampering Misdemeanor 5mo $6,500 Disabling security systems, bypassing locks, or interfering with protective measures without the owner's permission. Includes tampering with alarms, surveillance equipment, and access control devices on private or public property
CC-215 Littering Infraction — $1,000 Depositing waste in an inappropriate location
CC-216 Theft of an Aircraft Felony 20mo $14,000 Operates an aircraft without permission from the owner or lawful right to operate the aircraft.
CC-217 Theft of a Vessel Misdemeanor 10mo $7,000 Operates a seaworthy vessel without permission from the owner or lawful right to operate the vessel.
CC-218 Theft of a Gov. Vehicle Felony 40mo $25,500 Steals a Government Owned vehicle without the legal right to possess it. Government owned vehicles include police vehicles, fire trucks, ambulances, and military vehicles.
CC-219 Vandalism of Government Property Misdemeanor 10mo $7,000 Intentionally damaging or destroying government property.
Fraud
CC-250 Fraud Misdemeanor 10mo $26,000 The wrongful or criminal deception intended to result in financial or personal gain.
CC-251 Felony Fraud Felony 20mo $39,000 The wrongful or criminal deception intended to result in financial or personal gain from a business or institution.
CC-252 Forgery Felony 20mo $10,400 Creates false documentation, including ID cards, License Plates, Money, or Contracts.
CC-253 Impersonating a Government Official Felony 45mo $60,000 Falsely represents themselves as an official of any government agency in a manner that is reasonably convincing.
CC-254 Identity Theft Felony 10mo $15,000 The fraudulent acquisition and use of a person's private identifying information.
Weapons
CC-300 Weapon Trafficking HUT Felony — — Transports, possesses, or brings across state lines large quantities (16+) of unregistered, or improperly registered weapons/explosives. Firearm attachments will not count towards this. This is a Hold Until Trial Charge!
CC-301 Sale of Illegal Weaponry Felony 60mo $39,000 The selling or trading of weaponry or illegal firearm attachments without a valid permit.
CC-302 Manufacturing of Weaponry HUT Felony — — Creation of or possession of equipment to manufacture weapons (firearms and explosives). Equipment is considered a weapon manufacturing tool in addition to materials and weapon parts. This is a Hold Until Trial Charge!
CC-304 Negligent Ownership of a Firearm Infraction — $2,600 Fails to exercise reasonable care in securing, handling, or using a firearm.
CC-305 Criminal Possession of Weapon Attachments Misdemeanor 5mo $2,600 Possesses 1-4 illegal firearm attachments (e.g., suppressors, extended magazines).
CC-306 Criminal Use of a Firearm Misdemeanor 10mo $13,000 Uses a firearm against someone or carries it with intent to use against someone. Also applies to projectile weapons including antique projectile weapons. Does not apply to self-defense and does not overrule any other legal use.
CC-307 Criminal Possession of Explosives Felony 30mo $26,000 Possesses any amount of explosive devices, excluding fireworks, whether operational or not.
CC-308 Criminal Use of Explosives Felony 40mo $52,000 Throws, places, or propels an explosive into, against, or upon any real or personal property or uses an explosive device against a person/s. Excludes fireworks.
CC-310 Criminal Possession of a Class 1 Firearm Felony 15mo $7,000 Possesses 1-5 unregistered, improperly registered, or illegal class 1 firearms. Stackable up to 3 times.
CC-311 Criminal Possession of a Class 2 Firearm Felony 25mo $10,000 Possesses 1-5 unregistered, improperly registered, or illegal class 2 firearms. Stackable up to 3 times.
CC-312 Criminal Possession of a Class 3 Firearm Felony 35mo $13,000 Possesses 1-5 unregistered, improperly registered, or illegal class 3 firearms. Stackable up to 3 times.
Public Nuisance
CC-350 Desecration of a Corpse Felony 30mo $10,400 Moves or damages a deceased individual without consent from their next of kin, or without appropriate authority from the State.
CC-351 Inciting a Riot Felony 40mo $20,800 Incites violence as part of an assemblage of three or more persons.
CC-352 Unlawful Assembly Misdemeanor 20mo $9,100 The gathering of people intended to disturb the peace or commit an unlawful act.
CC-353 Loitering Misdemeanor 5mo $2,600 Remains in a place without apparent purpose.
CC-354 Vigilantism Felony 20mo $34,860 Acts as law enforcement without appropriate authority.
CC-355 False Reporting Misdemeanor 10mo $20,510 Reports a false or non-existent crime.
CC-356 Disturbing the Peace Infraction — $3,900 Causing a disruption or nuisance in a public space that bothers others. Must be weighed against social benefits and rights.
CC-357 Public Defecation Misdemeanor 5mo $6,500 Urinating or defecating in public that is unwelcome, disturbing, or vandalizes property.
CC-358 Misuse of an Emergency Line Misdemeanor 5mo $5,000 Uses an emergency line without proper cause.
Legal Process
CC-400 Failure to Obey a Lawful Order Misdemeanor 5mo $5,200 Disobeys a lawful order from a Law Enforcement Officer or Officer of the Courts.
CC-401 Resisting Misdemeanor 5mo $5,200 Flees from a police officer or resists being detained or arrested.
CC-402 Evading Misdemeanor 5mo $10,400 Flees in a vehicle from a police officer to avoid being detained or arrested.
CC-403 Escaping Felony 10mo $19,500 Escapes the custody of a Law Enforcement Officer or Police Department.
CC-404 Jailbreak HUT Felony — — Escapes from the custody of the state while being held in the confines of, in transport to/from Boilingbroke State Penn, or hospitalized for intensive care. This is a Hold Until Trial Charge!
CC-405 Failure to Obey a Court Order Felony — — Disobeys a court order. Time/fine is at Judge’s discretion.
CC-408 Obstruction of Justice Misdemeanor 15mo $20,510 Interferes with the discovery, arrest, conviction, or punishment of a crime.
CC-409 Bribery Misdemeanor 10mo $8,190 Offers a benefit to a public servant in return for beneficial treatment.
CC-410 Corruption Felony 30mo $52,000 Public servant or official abuses the authority and access of their office, through gross negligence or maliciously.
CC-411 Possession of Gov. Equipment Misdemeanor 20mo $39,000 Possesses or distributes government-issued equipment. Does not include equipment legally distributed by government employees.
CC-413 Voter Fraud Felony 10mo $18,200 Possesses someone else's voter registration or uses it to cast a ballot in an official election.
CC-414 Election Interference Felony 120mo $130,000 Unlawfully interferes with the conduct of an official election through fraudulent, deceptive, or coercive means, including tampering with voting systems, dissemination of false information, intimidation of voters, or obstruction of voter access. Voter Fraud alone does not constitute Election Interference.
CC-415 Conspiracy to Commit Election Interference Felony 300mo $260,000 Willfully conspires with others to unlawfully interfere with the conduct of an official election through fraudulent, deceptive, or coercive means. Cannot be combined with Election Interference. Voter Fraud alone does not constitute Election Interference.
CC-416 Evidence Tampering Felony 10mo $13,000 Tampers, conceals, removes, or transports evidence of a crime that can be used by Law Enforcement or the Courts to detain, arrest, or prosecute a person/s.
CC-417 Introducing Contraband into a Gov. Facility Felony 20mo $13,000 Introducing, attempting to introduce, or possessing items that are considered contraband within a correctional facility.
Commerce
CC-650 Embezzlement Misdemeanor 10mo — Uses trusted access to steal or misappropriate funds from a private business. Fine subject to amount embezzled.
CC-651 Felony Embezzlement Felony 25mo — Uses trusted access to steal or misappropriate funds from a public institution or state agency. Fine subject to amount embezzled.
CC-652 Money Laundering Felony 30mo $62,400 The concealment of the origins of illegally obtained money, typically by means of transfers involving foreign banks or legitimate businesses.
CC-653 Antitrust HUT Felony — — Found to engage in anticompetitive business practices that utilize illegal market manipulation tactics. This is a Hold Until Trial Charge!
CC-654 Practicing without a License Felony 25mo $60,000 Performs regulated professional activities, without the proper license
CC-655 Operating a Business without a License Misdemeanor 15mo $30,000 Operates a business without the appropriate licenses
Utilities
CC-900 Conspiracy Utility — — When 2 or more persons join together and form an agreement to violate the law, then act on that agreement.
CC-901 Accomplice Utility — — Intentionally and voluntarily participates with another in a crime by encouraging or assisting in the commission of the crime.
CC-903 Attempted Utility — — Takes one or more steps towards the commission of a crime.
CC-905 Bail Utility — — A utility charge to release an individual on bail until their trial date.
CC-906 Hold Until Trial Utility — — A utility charge to remand an individual to DOC custody until their trial date.

Civil Statute CS

CodeNameClassTimeFineDescription
Civil
CS-800 Tort: Breach of Contract Civil Statute — — Fails to follow the terms of a written or verbal contract.
CS-801 Tort: Negligence Civil Statute — — Acts without due care or attention, causing damage to people or property. The defendant must have breached a duty they had, and that the breach caused the damages.
CS-802 Tort: Malpractice Civil Statute — — As negligence, but within a license profession.
CS-803 Tort: Defamation Civil Statute — — A false statement about a person, that is communicated to a third party, where the defendant is at fault for at least negligence, and damages are suffered by the plaintiff's property, business, reputation, or profession. Statements about public officials require actual malice.
CS-804 Tort: Infliction of Emotional Distress Civil Statute — — Acts intentionally or recklessly in an extreme or outrageous manner to cause severe emotional distress through negligence or intent.
CS-805 Tort: Assault Civil Statute — — Commission of Assault against the plaintiff. Damages occur based on severity and cause of assault.
CS-806 Tort: Battery Civil Statute — — Commission of Battery against the plaintiff. Damages occur based on severity and cause of battery.
CS-807 Tort: False Imprisonment and Kidnapping Civil Statute — — Commission of the illegal restriction of liberty or movement against the plaintiff.
CS-808 Tort: Nuisance Civil Statute — — An unreasonable, unwarranted, or unlawful interference with another person's private use and enjoyment of his or her property. Must be weighed against social benefits and rights.
CS-809 Tort: Trespass of Land Civil Statute — — Intentionally going on land that belongs to someone else or putting something on someone else’s property and refusing to remove it.
CS-810 Tort: Trespass of Property Civil Statute — — Intentional unlawful taking or damaging of another person's personal property without the owner’s permission.
CS-811 Tort: Malicious Filings Civil Statute — — Maliciously abusing the court system, such as excessive and unfounded civil filings or abusive appeals, causing economic or emotional damages to individuals.
CS-812 Tort: Invasion of privacy Civil Statute — — Publicizing private affairs without legitimate public concern OR wrongful intrusion into a person's private activities OR releases private records inappropriately. Public areas grant no expectation of privacy.
CS-813 Tort: Fraud Civil Statute — — Commits fraud against the plaintiff.
CS-814 Constitutional Rights Violation Civil Statute — — An individual or group violating constitutional rights, enumerated or otherwise. Not used in conjunction with other civil statutes.
